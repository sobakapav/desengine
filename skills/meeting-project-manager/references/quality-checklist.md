# Quality checklist

Before returning the result, verify every item.

## Source fidelity

- Every decision is explicitly supported by the transcript.
- Every task has a clear source in the discussion.
- Proposals are not presented as agreements.
- No owner or deadline was invented.
- Ambiguous statements are marked as uncertain.

## Completeness

- The main meeting purpose is represented.
- Confirmed decisions are not lost among discussion details.
- Customer inputs needed to continue work are included.
- Blockers and dependencies are included when material.
- Previous tasks are reviewed individually when supplied.

## Actionability

- Every task starts with a concrete action.
- Duplicate tasks are merged.
- Large tasks are split only when independently actionable.
- The responsible side is clear where the source supports it.

## Writing quality

- Summary respects the requested character limit.
- Spoken repetitions and filler are removed.
- Language is direct, natural, and free of unnecessary project-management jargon.
- Internal and customer-facing information are not mixed.
