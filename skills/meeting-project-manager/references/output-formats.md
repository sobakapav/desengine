# Output formats

Use only the sections supported by the source and requested by the user. Omit empty sections unless their absence is itself important.

## Default internal format

### Краткое саммари

One paragraph, no more than 500 characters.

### О чём договорились

- Confirmed decision or agreed direction.

### Задачи команды

- Action-oriented task.

### Что требуется от заказчика

- Decision, material, access, or feedback required from the customer.

### Открытые вопросы и риски

- Unresolved issue, dependency, or risk.

## Previous-task comparison

### Выполнено

- **[Previous task]** — what in the meeting confirms completion.

### Частично выполнено

- **[Previous task]** — completed part and remaining part.

### Не подтверждено

- **[Previous task]** — the transcript contains no reliable evidence of completion.

### Изменено или отменено

- **[Previous task]** — what changed and what replaces it.

## Customer-facing format

### Итоги встречи

A concise neutral summary.

### Договорённости

- Confirmed agreement.

### Следующие шаги команды

- Team commitment that is relevant to the customer.

### Что ожидаем от вас

- Customer action or feedback.

## Compact task table

Use a table only when it improves scanning and the user has not asked for bullets.

| Сторона | Задача | Срок | Зависимость |
|---|---|---|---|
| Команда | ... | Only if stated | ... |

Never invent a deadline to fill the table.
