# Quality checklist

Before returning the audit, verify every item.

## Scope and evidence

- The audit states what was reviewed and what was not verified.
- Each finding has a precise visible location or scenario step.
- Measured behavior is not claimed without analytics or research.
- Reasoned risks are phrased as risks rather than facts.
- Customer comments and requirements are distinguished from direct observation.
- Competitors were searched only when explicitly requested.
- Every external competitor or current-practice claim is verified and cited.

## Finding quality

- Each card contains one main finding.
- Red, yellow, and green labels follow the defined meanings.
- Severity reflects impact, frequency, reversibility, and evidence.
- Green cards describe strengths worth preserving, not decorative praise.
- Duplicate local symptoms are merged or connected to a systemic issue.

## Recommendation quality

- Every red and yellow card has a detailed, actionable recommendation.
- The recommendation explains behavior, not only visual styling.
- Important states, transitions, or recovery paths are addressed when relevant.
- The recommendation does not invent unsupported business rules.
- Alternative valid solutions are acknowledged when the evidence does not justify one layout.

## Overall result

- The summary identifies strengths, main problem areas, and priority order.
- Highest-severity issues are easy to find.
- Counts are included only when meaningful.
- The result can be copied into Figma as text cards without major rewriting.
- Language is direct, natural, and free of unnecessary UX jargon.
